````md
# Excel trainer, still a student

At the end of 2020, I had already been working with data for 15 years, and with full-stack BI for 13 of them: ETL, data models, calculations, dynamic dashboards and automated reports, using Kettle (Pentaho), SAP BusinessObjects and Excel. I was even an Excel trainer.
````

### V02 · T2 Text+Scene · 22 words

````md
But I'm always learning. So over a few weeks, from December 2020 to January 2021, I went back to being a student.
````

### V03 · T2 Text+Scene · 74 words

````md
## Excel, the full stack

I finished a series of courses to build full-stack BI using only Excel:
- with Chris Dutton (Maven Analytics): advanced formulas and functions, charts and graphs, pivot tables, Power Query, Power Pivot and DAX, and "tips to go from beginner to pro,"
- and Kyle Pew's "Excel from Beginner to Advanced": pivot tables, Power Query, data models, Power Pivot, DAX, macros and VBA, advanced dynamic charts and much more.
````

### V04 · T2 Text+Scene · 27 words

````md
I also tried LinkedIn's new skill quiz and earned the Excel badge, in the top 5% of 7.5 million people. Not bad for an "old" Excel user!
````

### V05 · T2 Text+Scene · 43 words

````md
## Python, from zero

Next, I took the **Complete Python Developer** course from Zero To Mastery: scripting, data scraping, web development, bots and APIs, automation and machine learning. It was a well-structured course over several weeks, and the exercises and projects really helped.
````

### V06 · T2 Text+Scene · 57 words

````md
## Machine learning

Then came the **Machine Learning and Data Science Bootcamp**, also from Zero To Mastery. They broke every concept down to the basics, covered advanced topics, and explained everything simply. The final projects used big datasets from Kaggle, end to end: studying the case, preprocessing the data, modeling, evaluating and submitting to a Kaggle competition.
````

### V07 · T2 Text+Scene · 48 words

````md
My first exercise was to create, evaluate and improve a machine learning model in Python with Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib and Scikit-Learn. I finished it in an hour and a half, **without any copy-paste, just typing it all myself.** (That habit stayed with me. See [Just do the thing!](just-do-the-thing.md))
````
