# Find your community

I'm seriously amazed by the power of community-based learning.

People in good communities help each other. They solve each other's problems, and they share real-world wins (and lessons learned) from their own projects. And the leaders keep the content fresh, always adding the latest things.
````

### V06 · T2 Text+Scene · 50 words

## Why it works

- **You're not alone.** When you're stuck, someone has usually been stuck in the same place.
- **You learn from real projects.** Not only tutorials, but people's actual problems at work.
- **You stay current.** Good community leaders keep adding new content as the tools change.
````

### V07 · T2 Text+Scene · 23 words

````md
- **It keeps you going.** A leaderboard, a belt, a "great job" from someone you respect. Small things, but they push you forward.
````

## If you're looking to grow

Find your community. Show up. Follow the instructions. Help others when you can.

It worked for me in 2010, and it worked again in 2025.
