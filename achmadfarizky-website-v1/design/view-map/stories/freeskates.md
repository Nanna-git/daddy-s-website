````md
## What are freeskates?

My favorite pair is a little strange. They're called **freeskates**, and the brand is **JMKRIDE**.

If you've never seen them, you're not alone. Most people haven't. Freeskates are two small, separate platforms, one for each foot, each with two wheels. There's no strap and no boot. You just stand on them and glide sideways, a bit like snowboarding on the street.
````

### V03 · T2 Text+Scene · 26 words

````md
The sport started in 2005 with a company called Freeline. When it closed, some of its former team started JMKRIDE in 2015 to keep freeskating alive.
````

````md
## My collection

I have a few pairs: one for me, and some for my kids. I also have "wizard style" inline skates, a surf-skateboard and a scooter. But freeskates are still my favorite.

I skate in public parks. Sometimes with my kids, but most of the time alone. It's my way to take a break from screens, dashboards and DAX.
````
