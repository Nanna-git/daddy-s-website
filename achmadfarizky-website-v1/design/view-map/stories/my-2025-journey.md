````md
# 2025: from learning to a new career chapter

2025 was the year everything changed for me. Here it is, month by month.

**Late December 2024.** I decided to learn Power BI professionally. I already knew the basics, and I had years of BI experience with SAP BusinessObjects. I chose Power BI Park because it's project-based, not feature-by-feature, with many projects to build.
````

### V02 · T2 Text+Scene · 68 words

````md
**January–March.** I started posting my progress on LinkedIn, twice a week, after every project. This wasn't my own idea. It was guidance from the Power BI Park community. I started networking with other Power BI people around the world. Power BI Park taught me visuals, C# for Tabular Editor, TMDL, personal branding and an analytics mindset. It felt like a lab for experiments. Thank you, Prof. Injae Park.
````

### V03 · T2 Text+Scene · 41 words

````md
**April.** I joined the Fabric Dojo for one month to learn Microsoft Fabric, and I finished all the classes. Fabric was new to me, but data engineering wasn't: I'd been doing it for years with other tools. Thank you, Will Needham.
````

### V04 · T2 Text+Scene · 53 words

````md
**May–June.** I joined The Data Vis Forge to sharpen my Power BI front-end skills. This is where I learned corporate-style reporting. Gustaw Dudek, the "Power BI mentalist," teaches the techniques very well: not just the DAX, but naming conventions, using variables, and grouping measures. You learn a lot just by following his style.
````

### V05 · T2 Text+Scene · 49 words

````md
During these months, I also posted that I was open to work. My company had ended its operations, and I was ready for the next step.

**July.** I started searching for a new job seriously. I built a simple portfolio website, again following the guidance from Power BI Park.
````

### V06 · T2 Text+Scene · 38 words

````md
**August.** I had interviews with two big companies, on the same day, for reporting and analytics roles. **Both offered me a job.** My LinkedIn posts about my progress really helped. Thank you again, Prof. Injae and Master Gustaw.
````

### V07 · T2 Text+Scene · 55 words

````md
During the hiring process, I earned two certifications to build a solid foundation under everything I had studied: **DP-700** (Microsoft Fabric Data Engineer) and **PL-300** (Power BI Data Analyst). I had many mini projects already, but a certification is different. It covers the full picture, and there were many parts I didn't know before preparing.
````

### V08 · T2 Text+Scene · 66 words

````md
**September–October.** I kept studying, so I would be ready from day one in my new role. I passed **CHDA** (healthcare data analytics) and **CDMP Fundamentals** (data management). And in October, I started my "day one" at Johns Hopkins Aramco Healthcare.

**November.** I decided to focus on data management, because it brings all my 20 years of experience together. I started studying for the CDMP specialist exams.
````

### V09 · T2 Text+Scene · 14 words

````md
**December.** I passed the **CDMP Data Modeling and Design** specialist exam, at Master level.
````
