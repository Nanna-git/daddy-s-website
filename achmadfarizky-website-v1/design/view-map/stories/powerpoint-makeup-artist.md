````md
# PowerPoint: my first "Power Platform"

People talk about the Microsoft Power Platform: Power BI, Power Apps, Power Automate. For me, the first "Power" tool was **PowerPoint**. And I still love it.

Even today, I often choose PowerPoint over Figma or Canva.
````

````md
## An engineer who loves design

I'm a telecommunication engineer and a data person. But I really love graphic design too.

At university, I made designs for fun with CorelDraw, Adobe Photoshop and Macromedia Flash. My best art friend, Fakhrudin, was always creating amazing stuff too, and we pushed each other.
````

### V03 · T2 Text+Scene · 61 words

````md
Later, PowerPoint became my main design tool. I made flyers, book covers and certificates with it, all from scratch. No AI, no ready-made designs. I just loved making them.

That taught me something: pictures and design are very powerful for sharing ideas. And that skill helps my engineering and data work every day. **Our hobbies can make our main job better.**
````

### V08 · T2 Text+Scene · 65 words

````md
## Videos made with PowerPoint (and my family)

In 2016, I made education videos using only PowerPoint. I played the slides one by one, recorded my screen and my voice, and put everything together in Windows Movie Maker. For better sound, I used Audacity, a free audio editor.

It was a wonderful project with my family. It was part of how we did parenting together.
````

### V11 · T2 Text+Scene · 66 words

````md
In big organizations, it's still the most used tool for delivering messages. Our C-level executives use it in the monthly meeting with all employees, where management openly shares the main programs and the data. They also use it with shareholders. I was happy to contribute some design work there, and it got positive feedback. It was even one of the reasons I passed my probation period.
````

### V12 · T2 Text+Scene · 41 words

````md
The real question isn't how old PowerPoint is. It's how smart you are when you use it. And "smart" doesn't just mean artistic talent. It means **business context and a focused message.** Too much design can turn your message into noise.
````

````md
- Books that shaped how I design slides: *Storytelling with Data* by Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic, and *Everyday Business Storytelling* by Janine Kurnoff and Lee Lazarus.
````
