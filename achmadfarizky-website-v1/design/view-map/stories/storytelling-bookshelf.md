````md
# My data storytelling bookshelf 📚

## The Storytelling with Data family

In November 2025, a box arrived at my place in Saudi Arabia: four books by Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic.
- *Storytelling with Data*
- *Storytelling with Data: Let's Practice!*
- *Storytelling with Data: Before & After*
- *Storytelling with You*
````

````md
## From the books to the reports

So far, I've finished *Storytelling with Data* and I'm almost done with *Practical Charts*. I've already used many of their ideas in the reports I manage, and the results have been great.
````

### V08 · T2 Text+Scene · 62 words

````md
People who know me know how much I care about visualization, both beauty and effectiveness, in every project. I always treated it as a must, even when others saw it as a bonus.

Now, thanks to these authors and everyone who keeps promoting good data storytelling, I feel more confident to push this topic forward.

**It's not a bonus. It's mandatory.** 💪
````
