    Three threads in my story

    When I look back at 21 years of work, the same three lessons keep showing up. I'll point to them along the way.

    Thread 1· Learn
    1. Learn what you love, even if it's outside your job. I studied telecom engineering, but I also taught myself design and programming. Many times, that "extra" skill is what made me useful.
    Thread 2· People
    1. Stay close to people who see the business. Most of my best work started as someone else's idea: a friend, a team leader, a manager. They saw what was needed. I figured out how to build it.
    Thread 3· Build
    1. Build it cheap, with what you already have. I rarely had a big budget. Most of my solutions used the tools we already owned, with a small customization to make them do what we needed.
