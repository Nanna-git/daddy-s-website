Living like a local

The fun part of working and living in a foreign country is doing what the locals do.

Hiking with Hiking Taibah

In 2023, during my visits to Madinah, I started hiking with the locals of the city. For them it's a routine: they hike twice a week to enjoy nature and unwind from work.

My favorite trip was a two-day, one-night camping and hiking trip to Al Ajrad mountain, about 100 km northwest of Madinah, on June 2–3, 2023, organized by the Hiking Taibah Club.

We left Madinah after the afternoon prayer, set up camp, and started a 3 km hike before sunset. After dinner there was a cultural program, and then we slept under the moon. We woke up at 4 AM for the dawn prayer, had coffee and a light breakfast, and started the main hike at 5 AM: 8 km, easy to moderate. By 9 AM, we were on our way back to the city.

Wilderness first aid

In August 2023, I joined a wilderness first aid training with the same club, in Arabic, over two days in Madinah. The trainer, Dr. Eyad Ali Shannak, was one of the best trainers I've ever met. The material was very useful.

I really recommend this kind of training for everyone, hiker or not.

The Riyadh Bus

Back when I lived in Riyadh, the end of the summer holiday meant traffic jams on the way to work. So I tried the new Riyadh Bus. My first day was in August 2023, from home to the office.

It was much more fun than driving my own car, especially because the distance was long and the roads were crowded. Thank you to the government for such comfortable city buses.

What I learned

Living abroad can feel lonely if you only go from home to the office and back. Joining the local hikers, learning first aid in Arabic and riding the city bus made Saudi Arabia feel like home.
