Does a portfolio website matter?

Version 1: a few hours with Gamma

In the summer of 2025, Injae Park ran a class in Power BI Park about building a portfolio. As usual, I followed the instructions.

I built my first portfolio website in just a few hours, using the Gamma app, and connected it to my own domain: achmadfarizky.com.

It wasn't fancy. It was basically my CV, my community work, and links to my Power BI reports. But it was mine, and it was online.

Did it matter?

A month later, I had job interviews with two big companies. Both offered me a job.

I can't say the website alone did that. But I wanted the people who interviewed me to be able to know me better, even after the interview ended. A website lets someone see your work in their own time, without you in the room.

So, my answer: yes, it matters.

Version 2: this website

Back then, I wrote that my next goal was to build a permanent portfolio from scratch, with my own code and technologies like ReactJS and GSAP, inspired by tutorials from JavaScript Mastery.

The website you're reading now is that next step. And this time it's not just a CV. It's a home for my stories.

What I learned

  • Just do the thing. A simple website today is better than a perfect one "someday."
  • Learn what you love. My web skills from university, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Flash, came back again, 20 years later.

Thank you again, Prof. Injae, for another great class.
