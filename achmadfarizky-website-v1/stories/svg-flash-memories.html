SVG animation took me back to Macromedia Flash

I've been having fun with SVG animation in Power BI!

Injae Park and Guy Orlov recently showed how to make animated KPI cards. I built my own version on top of Injae's Power BI file, which mixes normal visuals with SVG. The result: a KPI card and a button slicer in one, with animation.

Hello, old friend

Making these animations reminded me of Macromedia Flash, which I used around 2002–2003, when I was at university.

Back then, my best art friend, Fakhrudin, was a famous animator and designer among the students. We spent a lot of time making things move on the screen, just for fun.

I really wish I had saved all my old Flash files! (Pak Fakhrudin, do you still remember Macromedia Flash?)

Fun, but useful too

My project is just for fun. But I think animated SVG, used carefully, can make corporate reports and dashboards nicer to use. A small movement can show the user what's clickable, or what just changed.

What I learned

Learn what you love, even outside your job. I learned animation 20 years ago because it was fun. In 2025, it came back to help me in Power BI.

Thanks to Injae Park and Guy Orlov for the inspiration.
