3. A year of trying (2009–2010)

Context
At the end of 2009, I left Telkomsel to build a career overseas. It took longer than I hoped. For most of 2010, I kept applying and waiting.

or press Enter

What I did
I used that time to fill a gap. After five years in data and reporting, I felt my telecom knowledge was not deep enough. So I studied 3G radio networks seriously. I wanted to understand how things really work on the business side of the network, not only the data and the reports about it.

What I learned

Thread 2, in a different way. To be useful to engineers, I had to understand their world, not just their numbers.

Patience. Some doors open late, but they do open. In October 2010, Ericsson brought me to Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

When I arrived, my English speaking was close to zero. My Arabic helped me survive, and my English just "grew by nature" for many years. (It took me another 15 years to finally fix it properly. That's its own story.)
